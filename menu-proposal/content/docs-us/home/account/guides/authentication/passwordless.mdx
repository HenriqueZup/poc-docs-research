> In this section, you will find information on how to login to the StackSpot Portal without a password.

<br/>

:::warning  

Passwordless login is enabled only for organizations that **do not use** the [**Single Sign-On**](home/account/organization/sso-group-mapping/index.md) method.  

:::  

Check below how to set up passwordless login for your StackSpot account:

## **Activating passwordless login** 

**Step 1.** Access the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com) directly; or, after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**;  

**Step 2.** Select the **'My Profile'** option from the displayed menu;  

**Step 3.** You will be redirected to a page with general information about your Profile. At the bottom of the screen, you can **activate or deactivate the Passwordless Login option**, as shown in the image below:  

![Screenshot of the StackSpot Account's Personal Profile section, highlighting the toggle button to enable or disable Passwordless Login with a red rectangle.](/img/passwordless-login-new.png)  

Now, you must complete the configurations on your next login. Follow the steps below:

## **Finishing the configuration**

**Step 1.** [**Access the login page on the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/login);

**Step 2.** Enter your email and click **Continue**;

**Step 3.** Enter your password and click **Login**;

**Step 4.** Add the 6-digit code generated by the **OTP (one-time-password)** authenticator;

**Step 5.** Click on **‘Set up passwordless authentication’** when the option appears;

**Step 6.** Choose one of the the access key options:

- Use a smartphone or tablet
- Your browser profile (e.g., Chrome)
- USB security key

**Step 7.** After registering the access key, you will be authenticated and redirected to the StackSpot Portal.
