> In this section, you will find how to change the language of the StackSpot Platform.

<br/>

You can change the language of the StackSpot Platform according to your preference. Check the available languages:

- Portuguese (Brazil);  
- English.

To change the language, follow these steps:

**Step 1.** After [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**;

**Step 2.** In the displayed menu, click on **'Language'**;

**Step 3.** Select the desired language.

You've changed the StackSpot Platform language.
