> In this section, you will find information about how to edit your Account Password. 

<br/> 

Follow the steps below to edit your Account password: 

**Step 1.** Access the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com) directly; or after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**;

**Step 2.** Select the **'My Profile'** option from the displayed menu;

**Step 3** On your profile's page, click **'Edit'** next to the title **'Personal information'**. 


**Step 4** Enter your new password in the **'Password field'**, confirm it in the **'Password confirmation'** field and click **'Save'**.
