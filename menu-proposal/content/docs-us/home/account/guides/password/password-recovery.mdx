> In this section, you will find information about how to recover your Account Password. 

<br/> 

:::warning 

- If your e-mail address was **registered through [SSO (Single Sign On)](home/account/organization/sso-group-mapping/sso-configuration.md)**, you **cannot reset** your password. In this case, contact your Account Administrator. 

- You have **five attempts to reset** your password. If you exceed this limit, wait **24 hours** to try again or contact your Account Administrator. 

::: 

Follow the steps below to reset or recover your Account password: 

**Step 1.** Go to the [**StackSpot Portal login page**](https://app.stackspot.com/login); 

**Step 2.** Click the **'Login with password'** button; 

**Step 3.** Click the **'Forgot password'** option; 

**Step 4.** Enter your e-mail address and click **'Reset Password'**; 

**Step 5.** Check your **e-mail inbox** for a StackSpot email regarding password reset;

**Step 6.** Open the email and click the **'Reset Password'** link button; 

:::caution

This email link will expire if not used within 24 hours.

::: 

**Step 7.** You will be redirected to the password recovery page. Enter your new password, confirm it, and then click the **'Reset password** button again; 

You will receive an e-mail confirming that your password has been successfully reset.
