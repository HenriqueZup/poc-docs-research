In this section, you’ll find out how to use your Portal homepage.  

<br/>

[**The StackSpot EDP Portal homepage**](https://app.stackspot.com/) was designed to offer full customization, allowing you to organize content and features according to your needs.  

This enables you to structure and prioritize items such as Studios, Plugins, Stacks, Workspaces, Applications, Infrastructure, and APIs efficiently, optimizing your workflow.  

Learn how to use, organize, and customize it below: 

## **Customize Your Homepage**:  

- Access your Studios, Applications, Infrastructure, and Workspaces directly from the homepage and reorganize them as you prefer. Adjust the order of the item groups by following these steps:  

    1. On your homepage, click the **‘Preferences’** button.  

    2. In the preferences menu, drag and drop the sections to arrange them in your desired order/position.  

    3. Click the **‘Save changes’** button.  

<br/>

Check out the steps in the GIF below:  

<br/>

![](/img/preferences-homepage.gif)  

<br/>

- Choose which groups to display on your homepage. For example, you can choose to hide Workspaces. Follow these steps:  

    **Step 1.** On your homepage, click the **‘Preferences’** button.  

    **Step 2.** In the preferences menu, click the **‘Remove’** button next to the group you want to hide, as shown in the GIF below.  

<br/>

![](/img/remove-item-homepage.gif)  

<br/>

## **Filters to Find Your Items**  

Use filters like **Favorites**, **My [items]**, and **All [items]** to quickly locate what you need.  

Here’s how to **select and favorite** your preferred Studios, Stacks, Workspaces, Applications, Infrastructure, and APIs:  

1. Go to the section of the item you want to favorite. For example, to favorite a Workspace, click on the **‘Workspaces’** section.  

2. In the item list, click the **‘More options’** button, as shown in the image below.  

<br/>

![Screenshot of the Workspaces section in the StackSpot EDP portal. A red square highlights the 'More options' button for a Workspace. The 'Favorite' button, represented by a heart icon, is visible.](/img/favorite-workspace.png)  

<br/>

3. Click **‘Favorite’**.  

<br/>

That’s it! Now you can find the favorited item on your homepage.
