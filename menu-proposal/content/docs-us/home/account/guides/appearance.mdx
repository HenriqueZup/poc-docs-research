description: Customize the Portal's appearance.

> In this section, you will find how to customize the StackSpot Portal's appearance.

<br/>


You can change the StackSpot Platform appearance according to your favorite theme. Check the options below:

- Dark mode;
- Light mode.

Follow the steps below to change the theme:

**Step 1.** After [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**;

**Step 2.** In the menu displayed, click on **'Appearance'**; 

**Step 3.** Choose your favorite theme.  

You've changed the StackSpot Platform theme.
