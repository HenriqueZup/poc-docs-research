## **Overview** 

You can view or edit some personal information in your account, such as:

- First and last name;  
- Phone number;  
- Email (view only);  
- Edit password and recover password;  
- [**Enable passwordless login**](home/account/guides/password/password-recovery.md): passwordless login is only enabled for Organizations that **do not use** the [**Single sign-on**](home/account/organization/sso-group-mapping/index.md) method;  
- [**Reset OTP authentication**](home/account/guides/authentication/otp-authentication.md).  

## **Edit your personal information** 

To view or edit your personal information, follow these steps:

**Step 1.** Access the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com) directly; or after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**;  

**Step 2.** Select the **'My Profile'** option from the displayed menu;  

**Step 3.** Ensure you are on the **'Personal Information'** tab in the main menu;  

**Step 4.** You will see a form with all the previously mentioned information. To edit it, click the **'Edit'** button;  

**Step 5.** Make the necessary edits and click **'Save'**.  

## **Update your profile picture** 

You can now add or update your profile picture on the StackSpot Portal.  

Follow the steps:

**Step 1.** On the [**StackSpot EDP Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/login), click on the **'Profile'** button;

![](/img/myprofile.png)

**Step 2.** You will be redirected to your [**Account page**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com). Click on the **profile picture icon**. Select your profile picture and click the **'Save'** button.

![](/img/change-icon.png)

Your profile picture has been updated successfully.
