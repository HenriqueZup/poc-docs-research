> In this section, you will find a guide on how to edit and delete variables.

<br/>

## **How to Edit Account Variables**

You can edit Account Variables in two ways:

1. **Directly in the Variables section** within the Account menu. In this case, you will edit the **Variable for the entire Account**;

2. If the Variable’s value is not mandatory at the Account level, it can be edited by the Workspace Admin who receives it to configure Contexts or customize it according to their Workspace.

<br/>

**Note:** In this section, you will learn how to edit a Variable you created within the Account.

### **Requirements**

- [**You must have Account Holder or Account Admin permission to proceed**](home/account/organization/access-management/roles.md).
- You must **be the creator of the Variable**;

### **Step-by-step**

**Step 1.** Go directly to the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com); or after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**.

**Step 2.** Select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu;

**Step 3.** In the Account Portal main menu, select the StackSpot EDP product and click on **'Variables'**;

**Step 4.** Select the Variable you want to edit;

**Step 5.** Click the edit button and make the necessary changes;

:::warning

The **name** of Variables **cannot be edited**. The information you can edit is as follows:

- Value;
- Set whether the Variable’s value is mandatory or not;
- Description.

If you want to change the Variable’s name, the recommended approach is to [**delete the Variable with the incorrect name**](#how-to-delete-context-variables) and create a new one.

:::

**Step 6.** Click **'Save'**.

## **How to Delete Account Variables**

### **Requirements**

- [**You must have Account Holder or Account Admin permission to proceed**](home/account/organization/access-management/roles.md).
- You must **have permission to delete Variables**;

### **Step-by-step**

**Step 1.** Go directly to the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com); or after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**.

**Step 2.** Select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu;

**Step 3.** In the Account Portal main menu, select the StackSpot EDP product and click on **'Variables'**;

**Step 4.** Scroll or use the filter search to look for the Variable you want to delete;  

**Step 5.** Click the **Delete button** next to the Variable. Then type **'Delete'** in the text field that appears to confirm, as the images below: 

You have deleted your Variable.
