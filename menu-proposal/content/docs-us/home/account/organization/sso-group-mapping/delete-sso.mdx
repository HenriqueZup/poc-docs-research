> In this section, you will find how to delete your account's Single Sign-On (SSO) configuration. 

<br/>

## Requirement 

- [**You must have Account Holder or Admin permission to proceed**](home/account/organization/access-management/roles.md).

Follow the steps: 

**Step 1.** [**Log in to the StackSpot Platform**](https://app.stackspot.com/login);

**Step 2.** Click on your **profile avatar** and select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu;

**Step 3.** In the Account's Portal main menu, click on **'Identity & Security'** and select **'Single Sign-On (SSO)'**,

**Step 4.** Select the SSO you want to delete; 

**Step 5.** In the left side menu, click the **'Settings'** tab; 

**Step 6.** Click **'Delete SSO'**.
