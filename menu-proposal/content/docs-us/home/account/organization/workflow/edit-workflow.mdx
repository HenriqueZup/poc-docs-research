## **Edit a Workflow Basic Information**

You can edit the name and description of a Workflow. This process is the same for Default Workflow and specific Stacks. Follow the steps below:

**Step 1.** Go directly to the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com); or after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**.

**Step 2.** Select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu;

**Step 3.** In the Account Portal main menu, select the StackSpot EDP product and click on **'Workflow'**;

**Step 4.** Select the Workflow you want to edit;

**Step 5.** Click on **'Settings'**;

**Step 6.** Click the **'Edit'** button in the upper right corner of the Workflow information screen;

**Step 7.** Make the necessary edits and click **'Save'**.


## **Edit Stacks of a Non-Default Workflow**

You can **associate or remove Stacks from Workflows** configured for specific Stacks. Check out the steps below:

### **How to Associate Stacks:**

**Step 1.** Go directly to the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com); or after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**.

**Step 2.** Select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu;

**Step 3.** In the Account Portal main menu, select the StackSpot EDP product and click on **'Workflow'**;

**Step 4.** Select the Workflow you want to associate or disassociate Stacks with;

**Step 5.** Click on **'Settings'**;

**Step 6.** Click the **'Edit'** button in the upper right corner of the Workflow information screen,

**Step 7.** Below the **'Apply Workflow to specific Stacks'** selection box, click the **'Add Stacks'** button.

**Step 8.** Search for the Stacks you want to add, select them, and click **'Save'**.

You have added more Stacks to your Workflow.

### **How to Remove Stacks:**

**Step 1.** Go directly to the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com); or after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**.

**Step 2.** Select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu;

**Step 3.** In the Account Portal main menu, select the StackSpot EDP product and click on **'Workflow'**;

**Step 4.** Select the Workflow you want to associate or disassociate Stacks with;

**Step 5.** Click on **'Settings'**;

**Step 6.** Click the **'Edit'** button in the upper right corner of the Workflow information screen,

**Step 7.** Below the **'Apply Workflow to specific Stacks'** selection, you will find a list of associated Stacks. **Click the delete button** (trash can icon) next to the Stack you wish to remove.

**Step 8.** Type the word "Delete" in the confirmation modal and click on the **'Delete'** button again.

You have removed Stacks from your Workflow.

## **Edit Actions of a Workflow**

:::caution
When adding a new Action to a Workflow, check if you also need to configure the [**Context**](home/account/organization/configure-context-account.md).
:::

**Step 1.** Go directly to the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com); or after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**.

**Step 2.** Select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu;

**Step 3.** In the Account Portal main menu, select the StackSpot EDP product and click on **'Workflow'**;

**Step 4.** Select the Workflow you want to add or remove actions from;

**Step 5.** Inside the Workflow, click on **'Actions'**, in the left sidebar menu.

**Step 6.** Here you can perform two actions:

- **To add new Actions to the Workflow**: click the **'Add Actions'** button and select the desired ones. Click Save.
- **To remove Actions from the Workflow**: click the **delete button** (trash can icon) next to the Action.

If you have **added Actions**, proceed to the [**'Context'**](home/account/organization/workflow/edit-workflow.md#edit-actions-of-a-workflow) section to configure their inputs for the Account.

## **Edit Context of Workflow Actions**

You can edit the [**Context**](home/account/organization/configure-context-account.md) of the Actions in a Workflow. 

That means you can define the Default values of the Actions' inputs across the account and set them as mandatory if required. You can [**configure the Context of these Actions at the Account level**](home/account/organization/configure-context-account.md). 

See how to do this in the following steps:

**Step 1.** Go directly to the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com); or after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**.

**Step 2.** Select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu;

**Step 3.** In the Account Portal main menu, select the StackSpot EDP product and click on **'Workflow'**;

**Step 4.** Select the Workflow you want to edit the Context for;

**Step 5.** Inside the Workflow, click on **'Context'** in the left sidebar menu;

**Step 6.** Click the button next to the Action you want to edit the context for and make the input edits.

**Step 7.** Click the **'Save'** button and repeat the process for all the Actions you want to edit.

You have edited the Context of the Actions in your Workflow.

## **Convert Default Workflow to Workflow for Specific Stacks**

:::caution 
When you convert a Default workflow into a specific stack workflow, stacks without any configured workflow will remain without one.
:::

**Step 1.** Go directly to the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com); or after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**.

**Step 2.** Select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu;

**Step 3.** In the Account Portal main menu, select the StackSpot EDP product and click on **'Workflow'**;

**Step 4.** Select the Workflow you want to edit;

**Step 5.** Click on **'Settings'**;

**Step 6.** Click the **'Edit'** button in the upper right corner of the Workflow information screen;

**Step 7.** Scroll to the bottom of the screen and select **'Apply Workflow to specific Stacks'** and click the **'Add Stacks'** button.

**Step 7.** Search for the Stacks you want to add, select them, and click **'Save'**.

## **Convert Workflow for Specific Stacks to Default Workflow**

:::caution
If there is already a Default Workflow in your account, you cannot perform this action.
For this, you first need to:
- [**Convert the Default Workflow to a Workflow for specific Stacks**](#convert-Default-workflow-to-workflow-for-specific-stacks); or
- [**Delete the existing Default Workflow**](home/account/organization/workflow/delete-workflow.md).
:::

**Step 1.** Go directly to the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com); or after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**.

**Step 2.** Select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu;

**Step 3.** In the Account Portal main menu, select the StackSpot EDP product and click on **'Workflow'**;

**Step 4.** Select the Workflow you want to edit;

**Step 5.** Click on **'Settings'**;

**Step 6.** Click the **'Edit'** button in the upper right corner of the Workflow information screen;

**Step 7.** Scroll to the bottom of the screen and select **'Apply Workflow as default for all Stacks'**.

<br/>

You have turned this Workflow into the default.
