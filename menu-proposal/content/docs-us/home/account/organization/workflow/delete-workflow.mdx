> In this section, you will find how to delete a Workflow.

<br/>

:::danger

This content is being deprecated. [**Learn about the new StackSpot Workflow**](create-use/create-content/workflow/overview/index.md).

:::


:::caution 
- **If you delete a Default Workflow:** Stacks that were associated with the deleted Workflow will be left without any workflow.
- **If you delete a Specific Stack Workflow:** Stacks will automatically revert to using the account's default Workflow, if one exists.

::: 

**Step 1.** Go directly to the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com); or after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**.

**Step 2.** Select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu;

**Step 3.** In the Account Portal main menu, select the StackSpot EDP product and click on **'Workflow'**;

**Step 4.** Select the Workflow you want to edit;

**Step 5.** Click on **'Settings'**;

**Step 6.** Scroll to the bottom of the screen and click the **'Delete Workflow'** button;
