> In this section, you will learn how to create environments in your account.

<br/>

## Requirements

> Permissions: [**If you have an Enterprise Account, you must have an Account Holder or Admin permission to continue**](home/account/organization/access-management/roles.md).

- **A StackSpot Account**
Access the StackSpot platform and log into your account.
- **Create and configure environments**
Configure your [**Environments**](home/stackspot/main-concepts.md) at the Account level. You lower the settings for your team to create Applications, use Stacks, and all the content available from StackSpot in your Workspace.

:::info
The settings you configure in Environments are **used by the whole company** in your account. The values you configure **do not communicate**, for example:
The **Dev environment** settings do not affect the **Prod environment**.
:::

## Create Environments

Please register environments when you use your Cloud account at StackSpot. Developers use environments to create content within Workspaces, such as Applications, Infrastructures, Connections Interface, etc., separately by development stages, such as development, quality testing, and homologation. Creating an environment for each or as many as you need would be best.

Follow the steps below:

> [**You will follow the steps below on StackSpot Platform**](https://app.stackspot.com/login).

<br/>

**Step 1.** Go directly to the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com); or after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**;

**Step 2.** Select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu; 

**Step 3.** In the Account Portal main menu, select the product **StackSpot EDP**, and then click on **'Environments'**;

**Step 4.** Click on the **'Create Environment'** button;

**Step 5.** Fill out the fields with the information below:

- **Environment Name**: Add a name for your environment. For example, Development, Homologation, and Production;
- **Description**: Describe your environment's goal.

**Step 6.** Then, click on **'Create Environments'**.

Your environments are now created. To configure them, [**set up the deployment in your Organization.**](deployment/deploy/overview.md)
