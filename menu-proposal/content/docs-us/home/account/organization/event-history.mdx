> In this section, you will find how to access event history.

<br/>

- Permission: Only [**Account Holders**](home/account/organization/access-management/roles.md) can **download** the Event History document.

## **About**

It is a **.csv** file. You can view data of all user-related events in your StackSpot account over some time.

You can check the following information in the file:

- The **responsible** for the action;
- **When** the action was taken;
- **What action** was taken; and
- What **changed** after an action.

### **Access Event History**

To access and download the file, follow the steps below:

**Step 1.** [**Log in to the StackSpot Platform**](https://app.stackspot.com/login);  

**Step 2.** Click on your **profile avatar** and select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu;  

**Step 3.** In the main menu of the Account Portal, click on the **'Identity & Security'** section, then select **'Event History'**;

**Step 4:** Click in the **'Initial date'** field and choose the start of the period you would like to see the events in from the calendar that appears below. Repeat the process by clicking on **'Final date'** to define the end of the period;

**Step 5:** Click on **'Export CSV'** to download the file.

Now, you can view the data of all events in the time you choose.
