> In this section, you will find how to generate, revoke and edit Service Credentials.

<br/>

> Only those with [**Account Holder or Sys_Admin**](home/account/organization/access-management/roles.md) permission can **generate** Service Credentials.

- You can generate Service Credentials at any time in the [**StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/login).

## **What is a Service Credential**

A Service Credential allows Enterprise accounts to manage service tokens with customized group and scopes for specific purposes. This enables organizations to perform pipeline-level actions without requiring direct involvement from individual group members.

These account-level operations include:

1. Creating Resources;

2. Executing specific tasks via pipeline without direct user involvement.

For example, you can generate a token with the following attributes:

- Name of a group called Tech Products;
- Permissions to only create and manage Studios;
- Assigned to groups responsible for Tech Products.

## **Steps to generate Service Credentials**

**Step 1.** [**Log in to the StackSpot Platform**](https://app.stackspot.com/login);  

**Step 2.** Click on your **profile avatar** and select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu;  

**Step 3.** In the main menu of the Account Portal, click on the **'Identity & Security'** section, then select **'Service Credentials'**;  

**Step 4.** Click on the **'Create Service Credential'** button;

**Step 5.** Complete the **'Credential Name** <!--and select the expiration time-->. Click on the **'Continue'** button;

<!--
:::info 
When a Credential is about to expire, you will receive an e-mail informing the exact time.
:::
-->

**Step 6.** Add Groups to your Credential. [**You can edit this step later**](#edit-service-credentials).

- Select individually;

Click **'Next Step'**;

**Step 7.** Review your information and click **'Save'**.

You've generated a Service Credential.

:::danger
Copy the following information:

- **Client Id**;
- **Client Secret**;
- **Realm**.

You **won’t view this data again** after clicking **'Finish'**.
:::

## **Revoke Service Credentials**

Permission: Only those with [**Account Holder and Sys_Admin**](home/account/organization/access-management/roles.md) permission can revoke Credentials.


:::tip

Before revoking a Credential<!--, or if the expiration time is near-->: **Create a new credential first and then revoke the current one**.

:::

**Step 1.** [**Log in to the StackSpot Platform**](https://app.stackspot.com/login).  

**Step 2.** Click your **profile avatar** and select **'Organization'** from the menu.  

**Step 3.** In the Account Portal, go to **'Identity & Security'** and select **'Service Credentials'**.  

**Step 4.** Find the Credential you want to revoke and click **'Revoke'**.  

**Step 5.** Enter the Credential's name in the confirmation field and click **'Revoke'** again.  

You've revoked a Service Credential.

## **Edit Service Credentials**

The **only variable** that you can edit in a Service Credential after creation is **Groups**.
Follow the steps below to do it:


**Step 1.** [**Log in to the StackSpot Platform**](https://app.stackspot.com/login).  

**Step 2.** Click your **profile avatar** and select **'Organization'** from the menu.  

**Step 3.** In the Account Portal, go to **'Identity & Security'** and select **'Service Credentials'**.  

**Step 4.** Select the Credential you want to edit.  

**Step 5.** On the Credential screen, you can: 

- **Remove Groups**: Click **'Remove'** next to the group you want to delete.  
- **Add Groups**: Click **'Add team'**, then select the groups you want to include.  

## **View Service Credentials**

You can **view all the Credentials** you have created on the StackSpot Platform.

In the same section where you created them, check:

- Credential name; and
<!--- Expiration time; and-->
- Creator of the credential.
