> In this section, you will find information about how to personalize and configure your **Organization settings** within your StackSpot account.

## **Organization Settings in StackSpot**

:::caution Caution!
If you are part of an account but **do not have permissions** to configure the Organization, [**go to the Profile section**](home/account/profile/index.md).
:::

Your StackSpot account is **shared across all products**: StackSpot EDP, StackSpot AI, and StackSpot Cloud. This means you can manage your organization's settings in one place, regardless of the product you are using. The account management portal has a **dedicated address: [http://myaccount.stackspot.com](http://myaccount.stackspot.com)**, but you can also **access organization settings** directly from the product portal you are using by clicking on your **profile avatar**.

If you have **Account Holder** permissions or are a member with management permissions, you can perform **general organization settings**. This includes **adding members**, **defining permissions**, and **controlling how the StackSpot Platform is used**. For example, you can establish rules for creating **Studios** and **Workspaces**, and manage how these resources are utilized by account members.

The StackSpot Account Portal is divided into two main sections:

- **Organization:** Where **you** can perform general account configurations if you have the [**necessary permissions**](home/account/organization/access-management/overview.md).
- **Personal Profile:** Where members can manage their personal information.

### **How to Access Your Organization Settings**

Follow these steps:  

**Step 1.** Go directly to the [**Account Portal**](http://myaccount.stackspot.com); or after [**logging into the StackSpot Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/), click on your **profile avatar**.

**Step 2.** Select the **'Organization'** option from the displayed menu.  

And that’s it!  

:::warning Attention!  
If you [**do not have permissions**](home/account/organization/access-management/permissions.md) to manage the **Organization**, this option will not be displayed.  
:::

### **Available Organization Settings**

Here are the main options you can configure in the Organization section:

- **Products:** Manage options for StackSpot EDP, Cloud Service, and AI products, each with its own settings.
- **Identity & Security:** Configure SSO (Single Sign-On), view Event History, and manage Service Credentials.
- **Access Management:** Add and manage Users, Permission Groups, and define Roles/Permissions.

:::warning Attention!

Users added to the company account **do not have permissions to configure the Organization**. This can change if the Account Holder **modifies their permissions**.  
To learn more, check the [**Access Management section**](home/account/organization/access-management/overview.md).  
:::
