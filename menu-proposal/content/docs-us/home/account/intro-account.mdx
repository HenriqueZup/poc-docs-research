## **Overview**

Your StackSpot account is **shared across all products**: StackSpot EDP, StackSpot AI, and StackSpot Cloud. As such, the Account Management Portal has its **own dedicated address: http://myaccount.stackspot.com**. However, you can still **access your account** directly from the product portal you are using by simply **clicking on your profile avatar**.

The StackSpot Account is of the **Enterprise type**, meaning it is **managed by a Company (Organization)** that oversees all primary configurations. This includes adding members, assigning permissions, and defining how the StackSpot Platform will be utilized. For instance, the organization establishes the rules for creating Studios and Workspaces and manages how these resources are used by Account members.

**Account members** can also manage **personal settings** and view details such as the permission group they belong to. For example, they can configure external connections, update their profile picture, change their password, and more.

The StackSpot Account Portal is divided into two main sections:

- **Organization:** Where Account Holders and members with management permissions can configure organizational settings.
- **Personal Profile:** Where each member can manage their personal account information.

:::warning Attention!

If you **do not have permission** to configure the **Organization**, this option **will not be visible to you**.
:::

Below is a summary of the available settings:

## **Organization Settings**

- **Products:** Includes menus for EDP, Cloud Service, and AI products.
- **Identity & Security:** Covers SSO, Event History, and Service Credentials.
- **Access Management:** Includes Users, Permission Groups, and Roles/Permissions.

[**Learn more in the Organization section.**](home/account/organization/index.md)

## **Personal Profile Settings**

- View your permission group.
- Configure external connections.
- Add or update your profile picture.
- Change your password.
- Manage access tokens.

[**Learn more in the Profile section.**](home/account/profile/index.md)
