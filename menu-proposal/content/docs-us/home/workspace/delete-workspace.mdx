> In this section, you will learn how to delete Workspaces.

<br/>


> Permission: [**You must have Account Holder or Account Admin permission to proceed**](home/account/organization/access-management/roles.md).

:::danger
Deleting a Workspace is an **irreversible** process.
:::

## **Requirements**

You can only delete a Workspace if:

- You are this Workspace **owner**;
- There is **no Application** created in it;
- There is **Connection Interfaces** created in it;
- It can have **no content** added in it;
- It cannot have [**any configuration**](home/workspace/configure-workspace.md) in it, e.g. Cloud Connected.

:::info
If you want to remove a Workspace with created content, such as Stacks, Applications. Or Cloud Account configurations among others, you must delete it before proceeding. You can only delete a Workspace if it is **empty**.
:::

If your Workspace meets all the above requirements, follow the steps below to delete it.

## **Steps**

**Step 1.** [**Log in to the StackSpot EDP Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/login) and click **'Workspaces'**;  

**Step 2.** Search for the Workspace you want to delete;

**Step 3.** On the left side menu, in the **'Settings'** section, click on **'General'**;

**Step 4.** At the bottom of the page, click on the **'Delete Workspace'** button. Then click on the **'Confirm Removal'** button to complete the process.

You've deleted your Workspace.
