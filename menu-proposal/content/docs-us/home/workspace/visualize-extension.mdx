> In this section, you will find the steps to view a Customizable Extension.

## **Introduction**

A Customizable Extension in StackSpot lets you create a new page within the StackSpot EDP Portal using an SDK. This SDK enables you to develop personalized pages that are fully integrated with the StackSpot EDP environment.

Extensions simplify tool integration, providing developers with a centralized and tailored experience. They can also access StackSpot data through a [**library available on npm**](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@stack-spot/portal-extension).

- **Permission required**: Extension viewing.

### **Steps**

**Step 1.** Go to the [**StackSpot EDP Portal**](https://app.stackspot.com/) and click **'Workspaces'**;

**Step 2.** Click **'Extensions'**;

:::warning 

You will only see this menu if your Workspace has at least one active Extension registered in your Organization.

:::

Done. You can now view and use the registered Extension in your Workspace.
