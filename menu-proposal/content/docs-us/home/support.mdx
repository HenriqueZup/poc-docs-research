> In this section, you will find how to get help on the StackSpot support.

<br/>

## **Do you need help?**

See our support channels:

- **Option 1** [**Access the StackSpot EDP Portal and log in**](https://app.stackspot.com/). Follow the instructions:
  1. Expand the menu with your user initials located in the upper right corner of the page, and click on the **'Support'** option (headset icon).
  2. You will be redirected to the Support page. Fill in the required fields with as much information as possible to facilitate the analysis.

![Image showing the StackSpot portal screen in the Support section with the headset icon.](/img/support1.png)

- **Option 2.** [**Access the StackSpot EDP Portal and log in**](https://app.stackspot.com/). Follow the instructions:
  1. Click on the **'Support'** button located in the left sidebar of the page (headset icon).
  2. You will be redirected to the Support page. Fill in the required fields with as much information as possible to facilitate the analysis.

![Image showing the StackSpot portal screen in the Support section with the headset icon.](/img/support2.png)

3. Access our [**StackSpot Blog**](https://www.stackspot.com/en/blog-en). You can chat with one of our team members.
