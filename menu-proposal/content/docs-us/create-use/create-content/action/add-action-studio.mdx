> In this section, you will find information on how to version, deprecate, or delete the version of an Action in the Studio.

<br/>

## **Versioning an Action**

To version an Action, follow these steps in your Studio using the STK CLI:

- [**Create the Action**](home/stk-cli/commands/action-commands.md)
- [**Publish**](home/stk-cli/commands/action-commands.md) it in your Studio.

## **Deprecating the version of an Action**

You can deprecate a published version of an Action in a Studio. When deprecating the version, no flag will indicate that the Action version is deprecated upon publishing a new version.

Users utilizing the deprecated version will receive an email notification.

To deprecate a version, follow these rules:

**1.** You need a profile with content deprecation permission;

**2.** You need to belong to the Studio where the Action version will be deprecated. Exception: users with the role type **Account Holder**.

To deprecate the version, log in to the [**StackSpot Platform**](https://app.stackspot.com/login) and follow these steps:

:::warning
Deprecating the version of a piece of content does not have a rollback.
:::

**Step 1.** In the Studio, navigate to the **Action** section and select the Action you want to deprecate;

**Step 2.** Within the Action description, click the button indicating the current version;

**Step 3.** Find **'Version History'** on the right-hand side. Click on the **'More Options'** next to the version of the Action you want to deprecate and then click **'Deprecate Action'**;

**Step 4**: Fill out the following fields:

- The **reason** for depreciation:
    - Lack of maintenance;  
    - Security failure;  
    - Bugs;  
    - Other

- The **slug** and **version** of the Action you want to remove.

To continue, click **'Deprecate Action'**.

You've deprecated a version of the Action.

## **Deleting the version of an Action**

:::tip  
If you publish only **one version** of an Action and delete it, the Action **is also deleted**. To delete an Action completely, delete all its published versions.
:::

To delete, follow these rules:

1. The Action version must not be in use or associated with any Stack.

2. Require a profile with permission to remove content.

:::caution
You cannot roll back after deleting the version of a piece of content. To retrieve the content, create the same version or generate a new one.
:::

To delete the version of an Action in a Studio, log in to the StackSpot Platform and follow these steps:

**Step 1**: In your Studio, navigate to the **'Action'** section and select the Action to delete;

**Step 2.** In the **'Description'** of the Action, click the button indicating the current version;

**Step 3.** Find **'Version History'** on the right-hand side of the screen. Click on the ***'More Options'** button next to the **version of the Action** to delete;

**Step 4**: Complete the following field:

- **Slug** and **version** of the Action to delete.

To continue, click the **'Delete Action'**.

You have deleted a version of the Action in your Studio.
