> In this section, you will find a guide on how to visualize the use of a Stack in a Workspace, Application and/or Infrastructure.  

<br/>

### **Version data**

To view Stack version data, deprecate it, or unpublish it, follow these steps: 

**Step 1.** Access your Studio;
**Step 2.** On the **'Overview'** screen, click on the **'Manage Version'** button;
**Step 3.** In **'Manage Version'**, you can view the status of the Stack versions in the following tabs:

### **Linked contents** 

To view the data of **linked content** to a Stack, follow the steps below:

Click the Stack you want to view and then on the **'Usage Insights'** section;

That's it! You can now view the content linked to a Stack.

Within this section, you can view the following subsections: **Workspace** and **No Use**.

### **Workspace**

You can see which Workspaces the version of the Stack is being used in. To check the usage details, click the Workspace you want to view.
Inside, you will find information such as:

- **Stack version**: Which versions of the Stack are being used in the Workspace?

- **Environment**: The Account environments where the versions are published. 

- **Used in**: Which and how many Infrastructures and Applications is the version being used? 

You can also deprecate or unpublish the version of a Stack that is not being used. 

To **deprecate** the selected version, click on the icon next to the version and then on the **'Deprecate version'** button. Now follow the steps below:

**Step 1.** Select the reason: Choose between **Lack of Maintenance**, **Security Issues**, **Bugs** or **Other**; 

**Step 2.** Type the word **DEPRECATE** in the text field to confirm the depreciation; 

**Step 3.** Click on the button **'Deprecate Stack version'**.

To **unpublish** the selected version, click on the icon next to the version and then on the **'Unpublish version'** button. Now follow the steps below:

**Step 1.** Type the word **UNPUBLISH** in the text field to confirm unpublishing;

**Step 2.** Click on the button **'Unpublish Stack version'**.


### **Not in use**

In this subsection, you can see which versions of the Stack are **not** being used in any Workspace and the date on which each version was published. 

It is also possible to deprecate or unpublish the version of a Stack that needs to be used. 

To **deprecate** the selected version, click on the icon next to the version and then on the **'Deprecate version'** button. Now follow the steps below:

**Step 1.** Select the reason: choose between **Lack of maintenance**, **Security problems**, **Bugs** or **Other**; 

**Step 2.** Type the word **DEPRECATE** in the text field to confirm the depreciation; 

**Step 3.** Click on the button **'Deprecate Stack version'**.

To unpublish the selected version, click on the icon next to the version and then on the Unpublish version button. Now follow the steps below:

**Step 1.** Type the word **UNPUBLISH** in the text field to confirm unpublishing;

**Step 2.** Click on the **'Unpublish Stack Version'** button.
