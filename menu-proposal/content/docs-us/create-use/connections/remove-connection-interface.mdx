> In this section, you will find out how to delete a Connection Interface you have created manually.

<br/>

You can delete a manually created Connection Interface completely from your account.

:::warning
To remove a Connection Interface completely, it **must not be in use**.
:::

To do this, follow the steps below:

#### **Requirements**

- You must have a [**StackSpot account**](home/account/index.md); 
- [**You must have Account Holder, SysAdmin or Account Admin role**](home/account/organization/access-management/permissions.md);
- The Connection Interface you want to edit must have been [**created manually**](create-use/connections/connection-interface.md#create-connection-interface-in-a-workspace-manually);

#### **Steps**

**Step 1.** [**Login to your Account on the StackSpot EDP Platform**](https://app.stackspot.com/login) and click **'Workspaces'**;

**Step 2.** Look for the Workspace where the Connection Interface you want to edit is located and click it;

**Step 3.** On the left side menu, click **'Connection Interfaces'**;

**Step 4.** Select the Connection Interface you want to delete;

**Step 5.** Click on the **'Delete Connection Interface'** button.

You've deleted a Connection Interface.
