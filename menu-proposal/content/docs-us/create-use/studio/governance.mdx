title: Studio Governance
description: Learn more about Studio governance.
---

On StackSpot, only the **Account Holder** and **Account** admin can manage some Studios. This is a possibility to handle the content in it. When a Studio has this management, if a Plugin or Action is published, it enters the governance flow.

The governance flow works for Plugins and Actions. Every time a content creator publish an **Action** or **Plugin** on governed Studio, you will see a **warning** and a **justification** on the terminal: 

- **Warning**: It is a message warning the organization has a Studio that goes through the governance process.

- **Justification**: The content creator needs to insert a **justification** when creating Actions or Plugins.

You can work with Studio Governance in two different ways:  

1. As a **Content Creator**: Creating Plugins and Actions;  
2. As a **Content Reviewer**: Analyzing and approving the Plugin and Action publication. 

## **Content Creator**  
As a content creator, you have to request to create [**Plugins**](create-use/create-content/plugin/create-plugin.md) and [**Actions**](create-use/create-content/action/run-action.md). But there are some things you can do, see below:

- View the request or justification history.   
- The justification for publishing the content and the content reviewer's comment.  
- View the approval status. 

### Follow the status of your request   
To follow the status of your request, see the instructions:

1. Log in on the [**StackSpot Platform**](https://app.stackspot.com/login).  

2. Go to your **Studio** on the left side menu;  

3. On this left menu, click on the  **Plugin** or **Action** section.

4. In each section, you will see a colored line in the middle of the screen indicating the status of your request. 
The status can be:

- **Pending approval**: The content is still being analyzed.   
- **Publication approved**: The content is approved to publish.   
- **Publication declined**: You cannot publish the content. If your content is declined, it will be deleted after 30 days.   
- **Pending creator update**: Here, you need to update or adjust content before publishing it. 

You can talk to the content reviewer through the **Discussion**. You can see it on the right-hand side of the screen.

:::info  
When you see a red notification dot above the **Discussion** button, it means there is pending content, activity, or comment.
::: 

:::tip
- Members of a Studio Team who is content creator can access the content, status, discussion, etc.

- Members of the Studio Team with a content creator role can send messages in the chat (Discussion button).
:::

## **Content Reviewer**

The review starts when the content creator runs the [**`stk publish action`**](home/stk-cli/commands/action-commands.md#stk-publish-action) or [**`stk publish plugin`**](home/stk-cli/commands/plugin-commands.md#stk-publish-plugin), and insert a justification.

To review the content, first go to **Governance** section, located inside **Studio** and **Account**.

The request for publication will generate a line on the **Analysis Queue**, in the **Governance section**. The statuses are:

- **Pending approval**: The evaluator will analyze if the content can be published or not.  
- **Pending creator update**: Here, the content creator needs to update or adjust content before the publication.   

Follow the instructions to review:

1. Click on the **'Discussion'** button, on the right-hand side of the screen;   
2. Write a message about the analysis;   
3. Click on **'Send review'** button and select options for review:

- **Comment**: You can comment on the thread. It's not possible to edit or delete any submitted comments.   
- **Request changes**: Request changes on the content.   
- **Approve**: Approve publishing the Plugin or Action in the Studio.  
- **Decline**: Deny publishing the Plugin or Action, and notify the content creator.

:::info  
When you see a red notification dot above the **Discussion** button, it means there is pending content, activity, or comment for your review.
::: 

- On the **History** tab you can see the status update of each submitted request. You can see in the following columns:

    - **Status update**: 
        - **Publication approved**: The content is approved for publication.   
        - **Publication declined**: The content cannot be published.
    - **Entity**: name and version.
    - **Type**: Plugin or Action. 
    - **Request date**  
    - **Studio**: Studio where the content was published.  
    - **User request**  

:::tip   
To download the zip file of the contents of the Action or Plugin, see the instructions:

1. Go to **Analysis Queue** or **History** subsection, in the **Governance section**;  
2. Click on the status you want see;  
3. After, click on **"Download content"**. 

In the downloaded zip file, you will have access to all the information related to the status of the content. 
:::

- On the **Documentation** tab, you can add an explanation about the account governance. Write more about what the content creator needs to do and more. It's optional. 

You can use the text editor to write your documentation. After doing this, you also can:

   - **Save draft**: save content to edit another time.  
   - **Publish**: after publication, you can also edit the documentation. To do this, click on the **'Edit documentation'** button.  
   - **Delete documentation**: remove the documentation.   
   - **Cancel**: it cancels what you wrote.  

In the **Settings** tab you can control which Studio will go through a Governance process. 

### **Enable and Disable Governance**

To **enable** or **disable** Studio governance, click on the slider in the **Manage Content** column, you will see it in the same row as Studio.

When you disable Studio governance and have **pending approvals**, there are two possibilities:
- **Approve all requests**   
- **Disapprove all requests**  

To select an option, follow the same instructions for review:

1. Click on the **'Discussion'** button, on the right-hand side of the screen;   
2. Write a message about the analysis;   
3. Click on **'Send review'** button and select option.
