## **Insights: Customizable Data Dashboard**

The **Insights** feature provides a customizable data dashboard where you can add or remove dashboards and explore charts related to entities such as Plugins and Stacks. Within the Insights dashboard, you can:

- **Visualize data** through interactive charts;
- **Apply filters** to charts and focus on specific chart lines of interest;
- **Remove charts** from the dashboard to create a tailored data viewing experience.

### **Available Charts**

You have access to charts with general information, including:

#### 1. **Apply Plugin**
- **`apply plugin` usage per month:** Displays the number of times the [**`stk apply plugin` command**](home/stk-cli/commands/index.md) was executed for each Plugin in a Studio, organized by month.

#### 2. **Apps**
- **`create app` numbers per month:** Shows how many times the [**`stk create app` command**](home/stk-cli/commands/application-commands.md#stk-create-app) was executed, categorized by the Starters of the Stacks in a Studio, per month.
- **`create infra` numbers per month:** Displays the number of times the [**`stk create infra` command**](home/stk-cli/commands/infraestructure-commands.md#stk-create-infra) was executed, categorized by Infra Stacks in a Studio, per month.

## **How to access your data**

Follow these steps to access the information in the Insights dashboard:

1. In your Studio, on the left-hand menu, click on **'Insights'**.
2. In the top-right corner, click on the **'Add data dashboard'** button.
3. Select the chart you want to view.

## **How to download data**

You can download a **.csv** file containing all the data displayed in your Studio Insights dashboard. To do this:

1. Access your Studio.
2. On the left-hand menu, click on **'Insights'**.
3. Click on the **'Download Studio Data'** button.

You can now analyze the data in the downloaded file.

## **Top Stacks**

The **Top Stacks** section highlights your organization's most relevant and popular Stacks. These are determined by the number of Workspaces and Applications utilizing each Stack.
