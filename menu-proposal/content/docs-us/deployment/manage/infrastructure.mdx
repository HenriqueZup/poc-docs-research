> In this section, you will find how the Infrastructure section works.

On [**the StackSpot Platform**](https://app.stackspot.com/login), you can track the status of Infrastructure Plugins after deployment. 

On this screen, you'll find details such as:

- Plugin listing;  
- Status of each Plugin

To find out how to access the **'Infrastructure'** section, follow the steps below.

**Step 1.** Access the [**StackSpot Platform**](https://app.stackspot.com/login);

**Step 2.** Go to your Workspace;

**Step 3.** Click on the **'Infrastructure'** subsection;

You can now track the status of your Plugin deployments.

A Plugin will have two statuses: **Success** and **Error**.

When an error occurs, you can view its log to understand and correct it. To do this, click the button **'Go to activity log'** to be directed to the [**Activities**](deployment/manage/activities.md) section.
