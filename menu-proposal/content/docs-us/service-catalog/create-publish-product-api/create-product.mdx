> In this section, you will learn how to create and publish a product on the StackSpot platform.

<br/>


Organize and group your published APIs by creating a Product. A product makes it easy for you to integrate APIs into your Partners' applications.  

Go to your Workspace Dashboard and follow the steps below:

**Step 1.** Access the **'Products'** subsection and click on **'Create Product'**;

**Step 2.** Under **'Basic Information'**, fill in the following fields:

- **Name**: Name your Product.
- **Description**: Describe what your Product is about.
- Logo (optional)

Then click on **'Next Step'**.

**Step 3.** On the **'Add APIs'** screen, click on **'Add APIs'** and add the APIs you want to your Product.

**Step 4.** Add the environments where you want to associate the APIs;

**Step 5.** Review the information and click on **'Create Product'**.

To publish the Product, click on **'Publish Product'**.

You have published a Product in the Catalog. You can add more APIs to your Product now.  

### **Unpublish an API or Product**

You can remove one or more APIs from your Product after publication. To do so, follow the steps below:

**Step 1.** Click on the ***'More Options'** button next to the API you want to remove. Then click on the **'Unpublish'** button;

**Step 2.** To confirm the API removal, click on **'Confirm Removal'**;

To remove a published Product, click the **'Unpublish'** button; then click **'Unpublish Product'**.
