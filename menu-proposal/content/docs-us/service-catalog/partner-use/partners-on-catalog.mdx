## **My Account**

In this section, you can:

- Manage your Account to choose who can access the Studios and Workspace.  
- Invite members to access the Catalog.  
- Configure Applications.  
- Customize the language and appearance of the Account.

## **Services Catalog**

In this section, you can:

- View the APIs and Products by environment.

### **Invite members**

Go to the **'My Account'** section to manage your Service Catalog Account.

You can invite Members to access the Catalog, check the steps below:

**Step 1.** Go to your Account, and access the Members subsection;

**Step 2.** Click on **'Invite members'**. Enter the email address of the member you want to invite;

> You can invite more than one member.

<br/>

Then click **'Invite'**;

![Image the account access. This page you see the members and can invite new ones.](/img/members1.png)

### **Partners**

To be a StackSpot Services Catalog Partner, you need to receive an invitation via email. After that, you can access the account.

After receiving the invitation:

1. Set your password;  
2. Login with your corporate email on the [**StackSpot Platform**](https://app.stackspot.com/login).

<br/>

In the Catalog, you will find details about each product:  

![Image where you can see the page access. This page is where you can access members and to invite new members](/img/overview.png)

- **Environment view:** You will find the environments available in the selection box on the left side of the screen. Its names follow the customer's standards set during the registration at StackSpot.

Each environment may have a different availability.

- **Overview:** You will find the list of available Products and APIs.

- **APIs:** You will find APIs published in the Catalog and available.

- **Products:** In this section, you will find the list of Products published in the Catalog.
