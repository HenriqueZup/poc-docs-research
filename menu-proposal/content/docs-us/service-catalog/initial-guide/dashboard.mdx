> In this section, you will find more details about the Service Catalog dashboard.

<br/>

In the Service Catalog Dashboard, you have access to three sections:

- **Overview**   
You can view APIs and Products created and published in the Catalog.

- **APIs**  
The section contains created and published APIs. You can also create and publish more APIs.  

- **Products**  
This section has published Products. You can also create and publish more Products in the Catalog.
