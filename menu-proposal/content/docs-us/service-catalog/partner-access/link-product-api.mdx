> In this section, you will learn how to link a Product or an API.

<br/>

You can link a **Product** or an **API** in the Partners account, follow the steps:

**Step 1.** Access your account on the [**StackSpot Platform**](https://app.stackspot.com/login);  

**Step 2.**  Click on the **'Partners'** section, on the **'Settings'** left side menu;  

**Step 3.** Access the Partner account;  

**Step 4.** To add a **Product**, click on **'Products'** and follow the instructions below:

  1. Click on the **‘Add Product’** button on the upper right corner of your screen;
  2. Select which **Products** and their **Environments** you want to make available to your partner;
  3. Click the **‘Add Products’** button.  

<br/> 

**Step 5.** To add an API, click on the 'APIs' subsection and follow the instructions below: 

  1. Click on the **‘Add API’** button on the upper right corner of your screen;  
  2. Choose the **APIs** and their **Environments** you want to make available to your partner;
  3. Click the **‘Add APIs’** button.

<br/>  

The services are now available in your Partners account.
