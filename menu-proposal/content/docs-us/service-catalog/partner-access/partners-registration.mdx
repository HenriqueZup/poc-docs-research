> In this section, you will find how to register a partner in the Service Catalog.

<br/>

> Permissions: [**You must have Account Holder permission to continue**](home/account/organization/access-management/roles.md).

You can invite companies to be your partners. They can create a **limited account and associate with your Enterprise one**. They can also access your [**Service Catalog**](service-catalog/initial-guide/product.md) in StackSpot. Each Enterprise account can invite **10 Partner companies**.

The Partners in this account have two types of permission:

- **Admin**: A person who can invite other members to be part of the account and use the products.
- **Members**: They can only use the products.

**The first person you invite to be your partner will be the account's Admin.**

To register and invite your partners, follow the steps below:

**Step 1.** Log in to your [StackSpot Platform account](https://app.stackspot.com/login). In the left side menu, go to **'Settings'** and click on **'Partners'**;

**Step 2.** Click on the **'Register partners'** button;

**Step 3.** Fill out the information in the upper tabs:

1. **Partner info:** Add your partner's name and logo, click on the **'Next step'** button;

2. In the **Register Domain** tab, enter your partner's domain:

- your-name@your-partner-email.com
- You can include one or more domains.

:::danger
In this step, **add all the domains** the Partner Account will use.
It is only possible to send invitations to members with domains registered here.

:::

3. In **'Partner account administrator'**: Add the account administrator to receive the first email. Fill out:

- First Name
- Surname
- Email

4. To finish, check all the information and click on **'Add Partner'**;

**Step 4.** Click on **'Add Products'** and choose which products you will make available to your partner;

**Step 5.** Then click on **'Add APIs'**. Choose which APIs and **'Environments'** will be available;

They will now receive an e-mail to accept the access invitation. The partner needs to accept the invitation to have access to the Catalog. 

:::tip
After registering a Partner, you need to link Products and APIs that the Partner will have access to. Find out how [**in this tutorial**](service-catalog/partner-access/link-product-api.md). 
:::

## **Partner account maintenance**

> Permissions: [**You must have Account Holder permission**](home/account/organization/access-management/roles.md).

You can see your Partners' account information in your StackSpot Enterprise Account.

There, you can access:

- Your partner's general information.
- The registered domains.
- Information about the account administrator.

You can change who the partner administrator is or restrict their access.

## **Change the Partner Administrator**

You can change your partner administrator. Follow the steps below:

**Step 1**: Log in to your StackSpot account. Click on **'Settings'** in the left side menu;

**Step 2.** Click on **'Partners'** and then **'Settings'**;

**Step 3:** Click on **'General'**. You can rename a new person in the subsection **'Partner administrator account'**. Add it by filling in the fields below:

- First name
- Surname
- Email

**Step 4.** Click **'Send invitation'** and wait for the new administrator to accept it.

## **Restrict partner access**

> This action is irreversible.

:::warning

You can only restrict a partner's access if you haven't given them access to APIs yet. If your partner already has access to an API, you must remove their access first.

:::

If it is necessary to deactivate your partner's access, in the same **'Settings'** section, follow the instructions:

**Step 1.** Under **'Settings'** in the side menu, click on the subsection **'Delete partner'**;

**Step 2:** Click on **'Deactivate partner'**.
