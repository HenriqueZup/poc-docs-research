> In this section, you will find how to grant access to your partner on StackSpot.

<br/>

After the Partner [**requested access**](service-catalog/partner-use/use-service-catalog.md) to an API, the user **owner** of the Service Catalog needs to review the request.

To review the access request, follow the steps below:

### **Access to the API**

**Step 1.** Access your Service Catalog;  
**Step 2.** Click on **'Settings'**, and then **'Access Request'**;  
**Step 3.** You will see three tabs:

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="Tab1" label="Partners Requests" default>
Here, you can grant or deny the Partner's access to your API.
 
To **grant** access, follow the steps:

 **Step 1**. Click on **green icon** in the **'Access requests'** section;

 **Step 2**. Add a **Client ID**;  
 
 **Step 3**. Then, click on **'Grant access'** button.  

To **deny** access, follow the steps:  

 **Step 1**. Click on the **red icon** in the **'Access requests'** section;  

 **Step 2**. Click **'Deny Access'**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="Tab2" label="History Requests">

You will see a **granted** history request in this tab.

Click on the **'Revoke access'** button to revoke access.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>
